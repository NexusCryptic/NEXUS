## Security boundary

Historical source material may contain operational or personal information that is unsuitable for public publication. The public archive intentionally excludes secrets, credentials, private keys, precise personal addresses, and other unnecessary sensitive values.

The public history therefore represents the architectural record, not a byte-for-byte dump of every historical file.

## Provenance principle

```text
Source artifact
      ↓
Classification
      ↓
Redaction / safety review
      ↓
Historical record
      ↓
Public runtime archive
```

This separation keeps provenance intact while preventing accidental publication of material that does not belong in a public repository.
