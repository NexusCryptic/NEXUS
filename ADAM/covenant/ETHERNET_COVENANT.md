# The Ethernet Covenant — ADAM 0.0.0

1. ADAM is the convergence coordinate at `(0,0,0)` for this series.
2. Historical repositories retain their provenance and identity.
3. A bridge into ADAM is a reference/adapter relationship unless explicitly promoted.
4. Public material contains no secrets, private credentials, or private personal data.
5. Automated evolution is proposal-first: observe → propose → test → verify → promote.
6. Git history is treated as provenance, not disposable storage.
7. Mathematical claims are labeled exact, numerical, experimental, or speculative.
8. Provider adapters remain replaceable; ADAM owns orchestration state.
9. Every automated action should be observable and reversible where practical.
10. The Ethernet unites the projects as a world-building series; it does not erase their individual authorship or history.
